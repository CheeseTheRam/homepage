Why did I Built This

This originally started when I was figuring out my sheep weights and how to manage them. My goal was for my sheep to weight 159 lbs at fair so I could get the most out of my lamb. When I was first taught about feeding sheep, I was told to only give 4% of the sheep's body weight. It's a good rule of thumb, but not always what I need.

I made my own formula for calculating my sheep weight and how much I need to be feeding it. I used this for about 3 or 4 months, which was fine. It took me about 5 minutes per sheep to figure out what my calculator does in a couple clicks

I was suggested by another brilliant family to look at online calculators. I saw one made by a state university, but it literally only calculated the rate of gain between two feedings. It's okay, if you know what you are doing. I already knew how to calculate that. However, it didn't explain what these numbers where and why they were useful.

That inspired me to create something that used my formula, explained my method, and did exactly what I needed it to. I had to jog my memory about coding since I used to be great at it, then I got very rusty in the past 5ish years or so. YouTube and W3S was very helpful and kind to me.

That tool ended up being very useful since I needed to keep track of it for my sake, and my family's sake because I already know what I'm doing, but I like to have conversations about things like this. It's hard explaining weights and dates and a bunch of randomish numbers, even thought I was keeping track of it! I ended up using this tool to figure out not only my sheep, but also my club member's sheep, and people who needed my help with sheep.

I figured out how to publish this to github. I was originally going to have it be a downloadable html app, but iPhones do not allow javascript and paperwork file exporting. I found out how to host a website for free privately under github. I hope to one day get a custom domain but its just a useless subscription to worry about all for the sake of prettiness. Luckily QR codes are free to make so I dont have to waste peoples' time.

Now that fair is over, I needed to make more tools because I had more kinds of paperwork to do. I figured I might as well polish everything and create a homepage. There are still some parts unfinished, but nothing man-made is perfect!

I discussed with my family about making my content available for public use. We discussed a name for my software. I was also suggested to have a buymeacoffee (coming soon as of writing this); so that I may pay for a proper domain one day, instead of a weird github one. As well as supporting my own sheep and livestock as I am going into college in a few years.
